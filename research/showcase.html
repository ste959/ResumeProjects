An Engineered Platform for Honest Strategy Research — and What It Taught

The durable output of the work is not a strategy; it is a tested system, a set of transferable lessons, and a clear view of where accessible edge actually lives.

Author self-directed quant research project System ~35 Python modules · 276 unit tests Sample 20 yr, 2006–2026 · US equities & ETFs
Abstract

This note describes a self-directed software platform for systematic strategy research, the empirical findings it produced, and how those findings direct the next phase of work. The platform is engineered around a single strategy interface and a shared evaluation harness, so every experiment is measured identically under a walk-forward protocol, an overfitting-aware validation gauntlet, a friction model calibrated against live fills, and a factor risk framework. Eleven strategies were run through it; none establishes a statistically significant edge on liquid, survivorship-constrained data, and each null is reported and diagnosed. The right reading is not failure but a located boundary: the work identifies where accessible edge must come from — implementation quality, less-efficient venues, and survivorship-free data — and the system is built to be pointed there. All headline results are confirmed on a pre-registered twenty-year hold-out.

§1System architecture

The engineering decision that makes the research trustworthy is a single one: every strategy implements the same causal interface and is scored by the same evaluation harness. No study can quietly relax the rules, and the two-hundred-plus tests — including one that asserts the no-look-ahead property directly — mean a result reproduces rather than merely being claimed.

§2Method

2.1 Protocol

The protocol fixes the assumptions that most often manufacture false alpha: walk-forward fitting with a one-period lag; returns taken in excess of the risk-free rate (the short rate moved 0%→5% over the sample); point-in-time data with survivorship control; and pre-registration of the out-of-sample window and hypothesis before the test is run.

2.2 Validation gauntlet

Backtesting N configurations on one history is a multiple-comparisons problem. Each candidate passes a battery of overfitting-aware statistics before any claim is entertained.

The power term is decisive: the smallest detectable Sharpe scales as SRmin ∝ T−1/2, so extending the record from six to twenty years lowers the threshold from ≈1.3 to ≈0.64 — and much of what a short sample calls a "null" is merely an underpowered test.

2.3 Frictions & risk

Realized trade cost is the half-spread, temporary market impact (the empirical square-root law), and a financing carry:

c = s/2 + κ σ √Q/ADV + b·(short notional)(1)

with κ in the literature range [0.1, 0.5] and stress-tested for sensitivity; a participation cap turns over-large orders into partial fills, making the backtest capacity-aware. Risk is structural — a factor covariance Σ = B F B⊤ + D driving a constrained optimizer, with tail risk from a Cornish–Fisher (fat-tailed) VaR. The cost model is not left as an assumption: real paper orders were submitted, and the model matched live quoted spreads for deep names (SPY, IWM ≈ 1.0×) while conservatively under-pricing a thinner one.

§3Findings

Eleven strategies were taken through the full apparatus. Consistent with an efficient-market prior on liquid, survivorship-constrained data, none establishes a statistically significant edge. The value is in the diagnosis: each result is reported honestly and each failure attributed to a specific cause.

§4What the research established

Ten strategies that "didn't work" are not ten dead ends; read together they are a set of transferable conclusions about where edge is and is not.

  1. The alpha boundary is located, not lamented.

    On free, liquid, survivorship-constrained data, nothing clears the significance bar. This confirms the efficient-market prior rather than a shortfall — and it pins down where accessible edge must instead come from: implementation, less-efficient venues, and better data.

  2. Forced flows pay the maker, not the taker.

    Every mechanical-flow strategy failed for one reason. A forced trade is the market's most predictable event, so its impact is competed away and reverts — the flow compensates the liquidity provider, not the taker who chases it.

  3. Implementation is the accessible lever.

    For a signal already in hand, realized IR is governed by the transfer coefficient — costs, risk, turnover, unintended bets — as much as by the raw IC. Without latency infrastructure, quality of implementation is where value is added.

  4. Most short-sample nulls are underpowered.

    Extending to twenty years cut the detectable Sharpe from 1.3 to 0.64 and turned "everything is null" into the sharper "the premia are real, but they are beta, not skill." Power must be established before a null is believed.

  5. Data provenance sets the question.

    Survivorship does not inflate uniformly — it destroyed the low-volatility factor (by removing the blow-ups its premium is paid by) while flattering momentum. What a dataset omits determines what it can honestly test.

  6. Honesty is a system control.

    Pre-registration, the test-enforced no-look-ahead property, the deflated-Sharpe gauntlet, and the decay monitor are not virtue; they are the controls that stop a research pipeline from shipping false positives — the way unit tests stop a codebase from shipping bugs.

§5Moving forward

The findings do not read as "stop"; they read as a prioritized roadmap. Because the platform is engineered as pluggable layers, each item below is a data or module change, not a rebuild — the apparatus is ready to be pointed at a harder target the moment resources allow.

Priority 1

Survivorship-free, point-in-time data

The single binding constraint. A CRSP/Compustat-grade feed removes the distortion that undermined the factor tests — and the platform's point-in-time universe and delisting machinery is already built to consume it, so every downstream result inherits the fix.

Priority 2

Intraday data → the execution edge

The one thesis with a live chance is liquidity provision — being the maker to forced flow, not the taker. It requires intraday/tick data, which the execution and market-making modules are already designed for.

Priority 3

Alternative & filing data

Informational signals orthogonal to price — insider transactions (Form 4), short interest, and filing-text sentiment — ingested through the EDGAR pipeline already in the stack.

Priority 4

Productionize the research factory

A signal-qualification screen that ranks a hundred candidates by information coefficient, decay, and turnover before a full backtest, plus a strategy registry — scaling the front of the pipeline the way the back is already built.

The durable asset is the apparatus, and the remaining constraint is data and infrastructure — not methodology.The system was designed to make the next question a matter of pointing it, not rebuilding it.

§6Limitations & disclosures

Survivorship bias
The free universe holds only surviving names, distorting the factor tests (§4); the point-in-time machinery awaits a survivorship-free feed.
Paper-fill optimism
Live paper fills carry no market impact or queue position, so the measured cost (§2.3) is a lower bound on true execution cost.
Single macro cluster
Even twenty years is ≈1–2 independent macro cycles; statistical claims remain modest and are stated as such.
Calibrated, not measured
Impact and borrow coefficients are sourced to the literature and stress-tested; the qualitative conclusions hold across the plausible band.
Contribution

The deliverable is not a strategy but a process, engineered and tested: generate a hypothesis, triage it cheaply, backtest it under real frictions, validate it against a live broker, and — the part rarely demonstrated — establish what is not an edge before capital is committed to it. That process, and the map of where edge remains, is what carries forward.
