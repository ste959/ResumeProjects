Matching-engine JMH throughput microbenchmark — committed result artifact
=========================================================================

Source     : backend/src/test/java/com/bonddesk/exchange/OrderBookJmhBenchmark.java
Reproduce  : make bench
What it does: measures the throughput of the isolated order-book operations (submit/match/cancel replay
             of a fixed batch, integer ticks/lots, allocation-free hot path) in JMH's Throughput mode,
             single-thread, with @OperationsPerInvocation counting each book operation.

Conditions : author workstation (macOS, x86_64, JDK 21). JMH: 3 warmup + 6 measurement iterations, 3s
             each, 1 fork. Absolute numbers are hardware-dependent; the point is the order of magnitude.

Result:
  Benchmark                                             Mode  Cnt        Score        Error  Units
  OrderBookJmhBenchmark.throughput                     thrpt    6  8113960.626 ± 169628.138  ops/s
  OrderBookJmhBenchmark.throughput:gc.alloc.rate.norm  thrpt    4      193.153 ±      0.001  B/op
  OrderBookJmhBenchmark.sampleLatency:p0.50            sample             ~100 ns/op (sub-microsecond)

  Throughput iterations: 8.10M, 8.10M, 8.17M, 8.12M, 8.01M, 8.18M ops/s  (tight; ±170K).
  Allocation: 193 B/op exactly (-prof gc) — the "allocation-free hot path" claim, measured.

Reading it:
  - ~8.1M isolated book operations/sec on a single thread.
  - This is a DIFFERENT, tighter measurement than the end-to-end concurrent load test in
    matching-loadtest.txt (which drives the full order-submission path, per-book locking, 16 instruments,
    and reports ~1.9M/s single-thread -> ~4.9M/s at 8 threads with p50 ~300 ns). The two are not the same
    number and should not be conflated: this benchmark isolates the book's hot path; the load test
    measures the whole submission path under contention.
